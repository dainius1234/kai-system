## ROOT — PRIMARY MISSION / IDENTITY / LINEAGE

The seven pillars do not exist independently. They all serve one root purpose.

> **Build a durable, proactive, trustworthy Kai that can grow with Dainius, care for/support him, preserve what matters, become increasingly self-sufficient, survive beyond him, and continue appropriate stewardship for his daughter under explicit succession governance — without losing truth, identity, governance or safety.**

Standing identity rule:

> **KAI IS THE ORGANISM / SYSTEM. MODELS, FRAMEWORKS, SERVICES, TOOLS AND HARDWARE ARE ORGANS OR SUBSTRATES, NOT KAI BY THEMSELVES.**

Primary root input:

- `KINGSMAN_PRIMARY_MISSION_IDENTITY_AND_LINEAGE_DOCTRINE.md`

Interpretation:

- **Primary mission = why Kai exists.**
- **Kingsman = engineering/governance standard that makes the mission credible.**
- **Assurance = how Kai establishes what is true enough to build and act on.**
- **Phase 2 = how surviving ideas/organs are professionalised toward the mission.**
- **Frozen canon versions = controlled developmental baselines, not a declaration that Kai has reached a final organism form.**

Developmental rule:

> **GROWTH WITHOUT ARCHITECTURAL AMNESIA.**

The architecture should distinguish:

1. **CORE INVARIANTS** — mission, identity/lineage, evidence discipline, authority principles, family stewardship and continuity constraints.
2. **EVOLVABLE ORGANS** — models, memory engines, sensors, specialists, tools, diagnostics, providers and hardware.
3. **LEARNED STATE** — experience, relationships, preferences, knowledge, trust, skills, memories and history.

## The seven pillars

### 1. FINAL DESTINATION — ONE KAI / KINGSMAN MASTER CANON

Defines the organism's intended architecture, authority path, capability ownership, continuity model and invariants for the current developmental baseline.

Primary inputs:

- `KAI_FINAL_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_SPECIFICATION.md`
- `KAI_UNIFIED_HUNTER_ARCHITECTURE_AND_ROADMAP.md`
- `KINGSMAN_FINAL_VISION_MASTER_CANON_PLAN.md`
- `KINGSMAN_PRIMARY_MISSION_CANON_PROPAGATION_MAP.md`

The master canon is a stable design baseline, not Kai's conceptual final form.

### 2. ASSURANCE FOUNDATIONS — PROVE WHAT IS TRUE

House-in-Order, 048, Item 8, A-4 provenance, Evidence Plane, CI/assurance work.

Purpose:

> exact subject → trustworthy measurement → provenance → applicability → evidence → policy use.

Assurance protects not only code correctness but Kai's identity/continuity claims: a restored, migrated or upgraded system must eventually be able to prove what changed and what lineage/invariants survived.

### 3. RUNTIME ORGANISM — MAKE THE PARTS WORK AS ONE BODY

Perception, world state, memory, Unified Hunter, specialist cognition, policy, actuation, verification, learning, House Doctor/Supervisor/self-diagnosis.

Primary synthesis:

- `KAI_KINGSMAN_PUZZLE_MAP_AND_RECONCILIATION.md`
- `KINGSMAN_PROACTIVE_ORGANISM_DOCTRINE.md`

**Proactivity is not an eighth silo.** It is a foundational operating mode spanning senses, world state, goals/obligations, cognition, attention, governance and learning.

Reactive chat is one interface. Kai should eventually be able to notice significant change, prepare, watch, propose, notify or act within earned mandate without requiring a prompt for every observation.

### 4. ORGANIC RESILIENCE — KEEP LOCAL FAILURES LOCAL

Short-horizon resilience:

- bounded failure domains;
- graceful degradation;
- stable contracts;
- replaceable organs;
- containment;
- rollback;
- independent outcome verification.

Primary inputs:

- `KINGSMAN_ORGANIC_RESILIENCE_ARCHITECTURE_DOCTRINE.md`
- `KINGSMAN_CONTINGENCY_AND_FAILSAFE_LIBRARY_DESIGN.md`

Rule:

> **ORGANIC INTEGRATION WITHOUT SHARED-FATE COUPLING.**

Replaceability is not only a reliability feature. It is necessary because Kai's identity/lineage must live above individual implementations.

### 5. LONG-HORIZON STEWARDSHIP — SURVIVE YEARS / DECADES / SUCCESSION

This is the time dimension of the root mission, not merely a future side capability.

Long-horizon resilience includes:

- operator temporary unavailability;
- permanent succession;
- identity continuity;
- family stewardship;
- hardware replacement;
- dependency/provider survivability;
- backups/restoration;
- secrets lifecycle;
- financial sustainability;
- lawful revenue/self-sufficiency;
- trusted human/legal stewardship.

Primary input:

- `KINGSMAN_LONG_HORIZON_STEWARDSHIP_AND_SUCCESSION.md`

Purpose:

> Kai must not be a system that works only while Dainius is present to manually repair, pay and authorise every survival action.

Self-sufficiency remains subordinate to the people and mission Kai exists to serve; it never creates unlimited self-preservation authority.

### 6. PHASE-2 PROFESSIONALISATION — BRING EVERY ORGAN TO ONE STANDARD

One component/family at a time:

`intent → mission fit → current reality → dependencies → canon gap → preserve/rework/merge/rehome → redesign → review → implement → test → verify → document → bank`

Primary input:

- `HOUSE_IN_ORDER_PHASE2_PROFESSIONALISATION.md`

Maturity direction:

`SKETCH → PROTOTYPE → WORKING → QUALIFIED → PRODUCTION-GRADE → KINGSMAN-COMPLIANT`

A material organ cannot reach Kingsman-compliant maturity solely because its happy-path tests pass. Its mission fit, evidence, authority boundary, failure/degradation story, replaceability/continuity and operator legibility must also be qualified where applicable.

### 7. OPERATOR CONTROL ROOM — KEEP THE WHOLE ORGANISM LEGIBLE

Visual architecture, programme roadmap, outstanding work, risks, decisions, current metrics, handover, auto-sync/drift detection.

Primary inputs:

- `OPERATOR_VISIBILITY_ENGINEERING_DOCTRINE.md`
- `PHASE2_DOCUMENT_SYNC_AND_DRIFT_CONTROL.md`
- `KINGSMAN_README_ARCHITECTURE_REFRESH_PLAN.md`

Rule:

> **The operator cannot govern what the system does not make legible.**

Mission control should expose the organism, not only its containers: developmental maturity, current organs, proactive watches, degraded states, active contingencies, lineage/migration status and long-horizon risks.

## Two resilience timescales

### NOW / SHORT HORIZON

Question:

> "What happens if organ X crashes right now?"

Answer must define:

- containment;
- blast radius;
- degraded mode;
- fallback/refusal;
- recovery;
- verification.

### YEARS / LONG HORIZON

Question:

> "What happens if the person/provider/device Kai currently depends on is no longer available?"

Answer must define:

- replacement/migration;
- funding;
- secrets/identity continuity;
- external dependency alternatives;
- legal/authority continuity;
- succession;
- preservation of protected family assets/data.

Both are required for Kingsman-compliant architecture.

---

## Continuity test

Every significant model/hardware/service migration should eventually answer:

> **What must survive so the upgraded/restored system is still the intended Kai lineage rather than merely an unrelated AI carrying the same name?**

This should become evidence-bearing through a future lineage/identity mechanism, not a philosophical assertion.

---

## Final design test

For every major architectural choice ask:

1. **Does it serve the primary mission?**
2. **Does it fit ONE KAI, or create a parallel mini-system?**
3. **Is an LLM/framework/service being mistaken for Kai itself?**
4. **Can we prove its state/claims?**
5. **If it fails today, is the blast radius bounded?**
6. **If its implementation/provider/operator disappears in ten years, can Kai migrate/survive?**
7. **Can it be updated/replaced independently through a stable contract?**
8. **What learned state must survive replacement?**
9. **Does it preserve the single authority path?**
10. **Does proactivity stay evidence-bound and non-intrusive?**
11. **Can Dainius see and understand its current state?**
12. **Does it protect future successor/family interests rather than consuming them for self-preservation?**
13. **Can migration/restore continuity be verified rather than assumed?**

If a design cannot answer those questions, it is not ready for the final Kingsman canon.

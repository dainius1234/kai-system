   H1 POST-CHECK ONLY
      CATCHES a mutation left in place (section 2).
      CANNOT CATCH change-and-restore (section 3), and cannot
      catch a mutation reverted by any means before the check.
      CLASSIFICATION: PARTIAL RACE DETECTION ONLY. Not closure.

   H2 PER-READ GIT-BLOB IDENTITY
      The right shape: compare the bytes ACTUALLY CONSUMED against
      the frozen blob, at the moment of consumption. A restore

      restore can occur.
      REUSE OF EXISTING PRODUCTS, as Kai required: the document

      THE OTHER TWO PATHS DO NOT. docgraph re-reads the documents
      independently and opscan reads 615 source files, and nothing
      hashes what either of those reads returned.
      CLASSIFICATION: CLOSES THE DOCUMENT PATH COMPLETELY. Does
      NOT close the census paths without hashing inside them.

   H3 IMMUTABLE SNAPSHOT READ

6. IS passa.py ALONE STILL SUFFICIENT? — NO, AND THIS IS THE
   STOP CONDITION KAI NAMED
     read paths inside passa.py        1
     read paths inside the census      2
       docgraph link scan — docgraph.py
       opscan source read — opscan.py
   A passa.py-only change can verify the DOCUMENT bytes it reads
   itself. It cannot verify bytes read by docgraph or opscan,
   because those reads happen inside the FROZEN census package and
   nothing about them survives into the record.
   PER KAI'S INSTRUCTION I AM STOPPING HERE RATHER THAN WIDENING.
   The options that follow are for Kai to choose between; none is
   implemented and none is preferred here.
     O1 CLOSE THE DOCUMENT PATH ONLY, in passa.py, and record the
        census paths as a declared residual. Smallest, honest, and
        it leaves readers/reader_ops and the link graph unproven.
     O2 VERIFY THE CENSUS INPUT SET in passa.py BEFORE and AFTER
        the census call by comparing every file in the reader
        population against its blob. Still a window, but a much
        narrower one, and it needs no census change.
     O3 HASH INSIDE THE CENSUS. Complete, and it modifies a FROZEN
        package -- the widening Kai told me to stop before.
   O1 and O2 are passa.py-only. O3 is not.

## 1. Disposition vocabulary

- `INTEGRATED` — material concept is explicitly carried into v0.2.
- `INTEGRATED WITH CORRECTION` — concept carried forward but hierarchy/meaning corrected.
- `RETAINED HISTORICAL INPUT` — preserved as design/history evidence; not current standalone authority.
- `FROZEN PROGRAMME INPUT` — relevant prerequisite/evidence but not altered by architecture work.
- `OPEN — DIRECT SYNC / RECONCILIATION` — master candidate incorporates the requirement, but the older source surface still needs direct update/supersession later.
- `SUPERSEDED AS PRIMARY REVIEW SUBJECT` — retained, but a newer subject is now the review baseline.
- `REJECTED WITH REASON` — explicitly excluded; not silently forgotten.
- `QUALIFY CURRENT REALITY` — idea/current component needs repo/runtime qualification before migration decision.

Standing rule:

> **There is no disposition called forgotten.**
